1. Draft and edit English text;
2. Organise tables and figures;
3. Check citation and reference consistency;
4. Verify reference metadata against Crossref and PhysioNet.

- The study design, data access, analysis and interpretation;
- Reviewing and revising all AI-assisted text;
- Verifying all numerical results against the project analysis files;
- Confirming the accuracy and completeness of all references;
- Taking responsibility for the final submitted manuscript and supplementary materials.

No generative AI tool was used to generate, modify or interpret the underlying research data or clinical results. No AI-generated images or figures were used unless declared elsewhere.
